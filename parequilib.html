From the founder

Why “Parequilib”

From para, alongside, and equilibrium, balance. Every marketplace claims to serve both sides; most quietly favor one. Parequilib began with a simple conviction: sellers deserve to present what they own beautifully, and buyers deserve to know exactly what they are getting.

“The fairest transaction is the one where both people understood exactly what they were getting into.”

Our fee is a transparent 10%, shown before you ever list. Soundline Studio is where creators publish manuscripts, music and files — free or for a price they set.
